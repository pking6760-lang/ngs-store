Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Nisha General Store delivers groceries and daily essentials locally.

Delivery area

We deliver around Sultanpur, New Delhi 110030 and nearby areas within our delivery radius. If your location is outside the radius, the app will show a “coming to your area soon” message at checkout.

Delivery time

Orders are usually delivered the same day, within a short window during our working hours. Actual times depend on distance, order size, weather and traffic.

Delivery charges

A delivery fee and small handling charge may apply and are shown clearly in your bill before you pay. Orders above the free-delivery amount shown in the app qualify for free delivery. A surge charge may apply during rain or peak times, and is always shown before you confirm.

Receiving your order

Please ensure someone is available at the delivery address and the phone number you provided is reachable, so our delivery person can hand over your order.
