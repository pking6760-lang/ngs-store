Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

We want you to be happy with every order. This policy explains cancellations and refunds.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order before it has been packed and dispatched — contact us as soon as possible. Once an order is out for delivery it can no longer be cancelled, as groceries are perishable and prepared for you.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

If any item arrives damaged, spoiled, expired, incorrect, or is missing from your order, please tell us at delivery or within 24 hours. After a quick check, we will replace the item on your next order or refund its value.

Refund method & timeline

  • Online (prepaid) orders — refunds are made to the original payment method. It typically takes 5–7 business days for the amount to reflect, depending on your bank/UPI provider.
  • Cash on delivery — refunds for a returned or missing item are adjusted against your next order or paid back as agreed with you.

What isn’t refundable

We can’t accept returns of items that have been used or opened without a quality issue, or complaints raised after a reasonable time. Perishable goods can only be refunded/replaced for genuine quality problems.

How to reach us

Email pking6760@gmail.com or see our Contact page. Please keep your order number handy.
