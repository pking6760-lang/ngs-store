Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

We want you to be happy with every order. This policy explains cancellations and refunds.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order before it has been packed and dispatched — contact us as soon as possible. Once an order is out for delivery it can no longer be cancelled, as groceries are perishable and prepared for you.

Damaged, wrong or missing items — 24-hour return window

If any item arrives damaged, spoiled, expired, incorrect, or is missing from your order, please tell us at delivery or within 24 hours of delivery. After a quick check, we will arrange to collect the affected item(s) and refund their value to your NGS Wallet.

Returns and refunds are only available within 24 hours of delivery. Once 24 hours have passed since the order was delivered, we are unable to accept a return or issue a refund for that order. This time limit lets us verify quality issues fairly, as we sell fresh and perishable groceries. You can return all of the affected items or only some of them — you are refunded for the quantity returned.

How refunds are given — your NGS Wallet

Refunds are credited to your NGS Wallet (store credit) inside the app — not back to your bank account or card. The refunded amount is added to your wallet instantly once we approve the return, and you can use it towards your next order (in full or in part). Your wallet balance and history are visible in the app under My Account → Wallet.

  • Applies to both online (prepaid) and cash-on-delivery orders — every refund goes to the NGS Wallet.
  • Wallet credit does not expire and can be combined with offers on future orders.
  • Wallet credit is store credit for use at Nisha General Store and is not transferable or withdrawable as cash.

What isn’t refundable

We can’t accept returns of items that have been used or opened without a quality issue, or complaints raised after a reasonable time. Perishable goods can only be refunded/replaced for genuine quality problems.

How to reach us

Email ngscustomersupport@gmail.com or see our Contact page. Please keep your order number handy.
