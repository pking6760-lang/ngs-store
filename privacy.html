Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Nisha General Store (“NGS”, “we”, “us”) operates the NGS online store and delivery service. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name and mobile number, used to create your account and contact you about orders.
  • Delivery details — the delivery address you enter, and, only if you choose to share it, your device location, used to deliver your order and confirm you are within our delivery area.
  • Order information — the items you buy, order totals and order history.
  • Payment — online payments are processed by our payment partner (Razorpay). We do not store your card, UPI or bank details on our systems.

How we use it

  • To take, pack, deliver and support your orders.
  • To contact you about your order (calls or notifications).
  • To run loyalty points and offers you opt into.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.

Sharing

We share only what is needed to fulfil your order — for example, your delivery address and phone with the delivery person, and payment amounts with our payment partner. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Data retention

We keep order and account records for as long as your account is active and as required for accounting and legal purposes.

Your choices

You can update your profile in the app, and you can ask us to delete your account and personal data by contacting us. Location sharing is optional and can be turned off in your browser or phone settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email ngscustomersupport@gmail.com or see our Contact page.
